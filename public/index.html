Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can an ambulance reach my location in Patna?

Our vehicles are positioned across 6 dedicated rapid-response stations in Patna (Bailey Road, Kankarbagh, Danapur/Saguna More, Ashok Rajpath, Phulwari Sharif, and Gandhi Maidan). Average arrival time is 8 to 14 minutes.

What is the difference between BLS and ALS ICU ambulances?

BLS (Basic Life Support) is ideal for stable patients, providing oxygen, stretcher, and EMT care. ALS (Advanced Life Support) is a mobile ICU with a ventilator, robotic CPR compressor, defibrillator, cardiac monitors, and ICU nurse for critical, intubated, or cardiac emergencies.

Do you provide air ambulance transfer from Patna to Delhi?

Yes. We arrange bedside-to-bedside air transfers from Patna Airport (Jai Prakash Narayan International) to Delhi (AIIMS / Medanta / Apollo), Kolkata, and Mumbai with specialized aero-medical doctors and flight ventilators.
